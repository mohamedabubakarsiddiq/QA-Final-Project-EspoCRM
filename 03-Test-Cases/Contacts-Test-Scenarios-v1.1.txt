Requirement: FR-CON-001

TS-CON-030 — Verify Followers
Requirement: FR-CON-030
Scenario: Verify a user can be added and removed as a Contact follower and Followed reflects the change.

Execution-based notes:
- Last Name is mandatory.
- Prefix is optional.
- First Name is optional.
- Starred email selection did not remain highlighted/indicated after saving and reopening; this is a confirmed execution finding to be documented in the Contacts Bug Report.
- Lock/Unlock and Audit Log were not available for Contacts and remain outside the current scope.
- Teams was available but no Team data was present.
